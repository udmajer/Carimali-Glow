# Carimali Glow landing page brief

Interview recovered from the completed planning conversation. Direct user wording is preserved where it was provided. The remaining points record directions the user reviewed and approved in the wireframe.

## 1. Vibe and references

User wording:

> "It should be more on this light grey tone. Beige is not really Carimali's color palette. It should be only grey and then this light blue glowy thing, and then use corporate blue as well. Corporate blue #013851 is the default brand colour."

Reference material: the supplied Glow presentation, official product photography, Higgsfield atmosphere images, Carimali positive and negative logos, and the approved low-fidelity wireframe.

## 2. Scroll journey

Approved sequence:

1. Glow appears in a split-stage hero controlled by scroll.
2. The machine moves into a real café or bar setting.
3. A short Milan 1919 section establishes Carimali's Italian origin.
4. Real close-ups show barista controls.
5. Six official frames prove performance, opening with the cool-touch steam wands and ending with the insulated boiler as the peak.
6. The 1GR, 2GR and 3GR lineup appears once, followed directly by 2GR specifications.
7. The complete 2GR returns in a café setting beside the quote form.

## 3. Energy curve

Approved direction: calm and controlled at entry, warmer in the venue, quiet and authoritative at 1919, more active through the control rail, most intense at the boiler reveal, then calm and clear through the range and quote decision.

## 4. Feeling curve and memorable moment

1. **Confidence.** The machine comes forward under the visitor's hand while the headline remains clear.
2. **Desire.** Glow sits in a venue that feels achievable for a café, bakery, restaurant bar or hotel lounge.
3. **Trust.** A restrained corporate-blue field connects Milan in 1919 to more than a century of coffee-machine development.
4. **Control.** Real display, volumetric-control and work-area photographs travel laterally.
5. **Assurance.** Six proof frames advance inside a held stage; the 11 L insulated boiler receives the longest pause.
6. **Clarity.** The lineup establishes equal machine height and increasing width from 1GR to 3GR; the 2GR remains dominant through the specification table without a second, repetitive product panel.
7. **Certainty.** The real 2GR returns in a café setting as the buyer completes a quote request.

## 5. One thing the page should do differently

Approved signature move: a thin light-blue line based on Glow's illuminated side seam draws a continuous path through the page. It passes through each proof point, wraps the boiler at the performance peak, then ends on the real side-panel seam in the final machine image.

## 6. Aesthetic range

Premium-minimal, but in Carimali's cool industrial palette rather than beige or luxury cream. Cool light grey, charcoal, corporate blue `#013851`, white on dark, and one narrow light-blue luminous accent.

## 7. World structure

Distinct scenes, joined by the continuous seam line. The page is a sequence of product moments rather than one unbroken camera flight.

## 8. Existing assets

The user supplied:

- A dense-keyframe hero scrub video and matching poster
- A native portrait hero still
- One counter-presence atmosphere image
- Three real barista-control close-ups
- Six official performance proof frames
- A real 1GR, 2GR and 3GR lineup
- A real 2GR front image
- A portrait café hero image reused as the closing product scene
- Positive and negative Carimali logos

No generated media or paid generation is required for this build.

## Audience and belief

Primary audience: independent cafés, bakeries and kiosks.

Secondary audience: restaurant bars and hotel lounges.

The page must leave the visitor with one belief:

> Carimali Glow gives my team the capacity and control of a professional two-group machine in a design that fits my counter.

## Conversion action

One label everywhere: **Get pricing**.

## Feeling peak

Visitor description:

> The blue seam travelled through the machine, lit the 11-litre boiler, and finished on the side of the real Glow.

Peak act: Performance proof.

The peak receives the largest scroll span and the longest hold on the insulated-boiler frame. The range section that follows is deliberately quieter.

## Tell-someone sentence

> It's the site where Glow's blue seam guides you through the machine, lights the boiler, and lands on the real product.

## Authored silence

The closing note in the barista-control rail reduces image density and movement before the performance proof begins. It is a deliberate pause, not dead scroll.

## Page grammar

**Filmic one-shot**, adapted to distinct photographic scenes joined by one drawn seam.

Why it fits: the page installs one linear sales belief, uses one conversion action, and already has a scrub hero, pinned proof sequence and continuous visual thread.

Why the other seven grammars lost:

- Chaptered editorial would make the product story read like an article.
- Live surface is for software or an operable interface.
- Continuous world conflicts with the approved distinct scenes and supplied media.
- Typographic poster would waste the strongest product assets.
- Gallery or catalog would give the 1GR and 3GR too much weight relative to the 2GR.
- Split stage requires two competing sides throughout the page; Glow has one product argument.
- Rhythmic cutlist would rush a professional B2B purchase.

## Scroll score

| Beat | Feeling | Device | Planned span | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | Confidence | Scrub + one kinetic headline | 2.2 vh | The supplied clip gives the visitor direct control of the opening product reveal. |
| Counter presence | Desire | Flow + full-frame reveal | 0.8 vh | A clean cut into a venue changes context without another held stage. |
| Italian heritage | Trust | Short pin + seam draw | 1.2 vh | The frame holds long enough for 1919 and Milan to register. |
| Barista control | Control | Interactive hotspot pin | 2.4 vh | One official image becomes a guided inspection of dosing, extraction timing and the work area. |
| Performance proof | Assurance | Pin + bespoke six-frame advance | 3.5 vh | This is the peak and gets the largest span. |
| Range and specifications | Clarity | Horizontal pan | 2.2 vh | The lineup reads as a choice while the 2GR specification panel remains dominant. |
| Commitment | Certainty | Flow + sticky product/form split | 1.2 vh | Normal document flow makes every field keyboard-reachable and lets the ending hold. |

Planned total: approximately 13.5 viewport-heights.

Device sequence: `scrub → reveal → pin → hotspot pin → proof pin → pan → flow`. The three held acts have deliberately different jobs: heritage establishes trust, the hotspot stage invites product inspection, and performance delivers the technical peak.

## Fingerprint gate

The registry is empty. This first build clears the gate automatically.

Planned fingerprint:

- Grammar: Filmic one-shot
- Nav: Fixed cool-grey Carimali bar with one quote action
- Hero: Split-stage product scrub on desktop, portrait still on mobile
- Sequence: Seven acts, about 13.5 vh, scrub → reveal → pin → hotspot pin → proof pin → pan → flow
- Close: Sticky café-product and live form split, no magnetic CTA or spotlight
- Signature: A page-spanning seam path that wraps the boiler and ends on the real side panel

## Publishing guardrails

- Do not show the list price.
- Do not introduce grinders, installation packages or accessories.
- Do not use unapproved testimonials, reliability rankings or certification badges.
- Keep 1GR and 3GR secondary to the 2GR.
- Confirm local electrical choices and product availability before publication.
- Confirm the public site's exact origin is present in VEA's `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` allowlist before launch.

## Post-build feel check

Cold-scroll words: **confident → tangible → rooted → controlled → assured → clear → ready**.

This matches the intended curve. The performance act is the largest change in visual density and has the longest scroll span; the insulated-boiler frame holds longest. The final product-and-form split resolves into a clear decision instead of fading out.
